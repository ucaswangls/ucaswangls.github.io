I am **Lishun Wang**, a Distinguished Research Fellow at Southwest Petroleum University and a member of the Image Processing and Parallel Computing (IPPC) Laboratory. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Software and Theory from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences and was selected for the young talent program of Sichuan's Tianfu EM Plan.

My research focuses on intelligent imaging, efficient multimodal large models, and embodied intelligence. I lead several research projects, including a National Natural Science Foundation of China Young Scientists Fund project (Category C) and a Sichuan Natural Science Foundation Young Scientists Fund project (Category B). I also contribute to national research programs, including the National Key R&D Program of China.

I have published more than 20 papers in leading artificial intelligence and optics journals and conferences, including IEEE TPAMI, IJCV, Photonics Research, CVPR, ICCV, and ECCV. I hold several granted Chinese invention patents, with research outcomes transferred into practical applications, and serve as a reviewer for major international journals and conferences.

## Research interests

Intelligent imaging, 3D reconstruction, efficient multimodal large models, and embodied intelligence.
